Controller Firmware Update

This will roll a new version of software onto the server. To accomplish this you must have a computer that can join your network and the appropriate controller device.
To use you need to:
 1 - Receive a new version of the software from the developer in a '.bin' format and place it on your computer
 2 - Place both your computer with the .bin file on it and your controller device on the same WiFi network
 3 - In a supported browser go to 'yourSeverIPAddress'/Update.
 4 - Click on the 'Choose File' button and choose the .bin file you received from the developer.
 5 - Click on the 'Update' button.

When the update is complete, the server will reboot automatically and your progress bar will show 100%.
